orcho-v7 expands to 11 stores by round 100 and stays there. mlp-v10 and mlp-v10-rl stall at 5-6 stores in this field (the notes describe up to 12; they were tuned in fields with other opponents). Everyone starts at 4 stores by round 5.

Staff and cash

orcho-v7 keeps employee happiness at about 0.84 with wage near 0.67. mlp-v10 averages happiness 0.16 and mlp-v10-rl 0.28 even though they spend more on wage late: in the losing matches they ran deep into debt (min cash about -214k and -226k) and unpaid wages sank morale, the spiral described in sac-v2's notes. orcho-v7 also hit -213k in its one losing match.

Caveats

  • 6 matches, one world seed, same six-agent lineup every time; the rotation sets the home region, and results swing with it.
  • The Krischo notes report 500k-660k for v10 in other fields; this field (with orcho-v7, an aggressive 11-store opponent) is harder for it.
  • Expansion order was not extracted: the log's opened column was not reliable enough to reconstruct it.
